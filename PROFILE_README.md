## Featured projects

### 🎓 Gen-Z College Admission Predictor

`Python` · `scikit-learn` · `XGBoost` · `Flask` · `pandas`

**Problem.** Predict whether a college applicant is admitted from academic, extracurricular, and lifestyle factors — on a million-row dataset where 88% of applicants are admitted, so a model that predicts "admit" every time already looks 88% accurate and is useless.

**Solution.** A modular, production-style ML pipeline — data ingestion, transformation, model training, and hyperparameter tuning — wrapped in a Flask web interface. Class weighting and stratified splitting handle the imbalance, and evaluation prioritises ROC-AUC and minority-class recall over raw accuracy.

**Impact.**
- Trained and benchmarked **5 models** on **1,000,000 rows across 20 features**
- **Logistic Regression won at 0.8805 ROC-AUC**, edging out XGBoost, HistGradientBoosting, Random Forest, and Decision Tree
- Held **0.81 recall on the minority (non-admit) class** despite an 88/12 split
- Served through a Flask app with saved model and preprocessor artifacts

🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/Priyank0302/genz-admission)

### 🎨 GAN Image Generation

`Python` · `TensorFlow` · `Keras` · `GANs`

**Problem.** GANs are notoriously hard to train — the generator and discriminator have to improve in balance, and it's easy to collapse to a single output long before the images look real.

**Solution.** Built and trained generative adversarial networks from scratch across two domains — anime-style artwork and MNIST handwritten digits — in TensorFlow and Keras, tuning generator/discriminator architecture for image quality and training stability.

**Highlights.**
- Implemented **separate GAN architectures** for anime art and MNIST digits
- Focused on **stabilising training** and avoiding mode collapse
- Hands-on exploration of generator/discriminator design, loss balancing, and tuning

🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/Priyank0302/GAN_Project)
